## About this site

This portfolio is styled as a painting in a frame. Each hotspot on the cover
opens a page with its own visual language, meant to evoke the place the project
was made.

## Interests

- Analog photography and the patience it demands.
- Long walks with no particular destination.
- Typography, and the endless argument about serifs.

## Contact

The best way to reach me is by email. Links to code and writing live elsewhere
and can be added here once they are ready.

## Colophon

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. Markdown pages are parsed
at build time and laid out in a single or double column depending on the width
of the page.
